FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need a roof replacement or just a repair?

If your roof is over 20 years old, has multiple leaks, or shows widespread damage like missing shingles and sagging, a replacement is often the best long-term investment. We offer free inspections to give you an honest assessment—no pressure to commit.

Can you help me with an insurance claim for storm damage?

Absolutely. We work with insurance companies regularly and can help guide you through the claims process from start to finish. We'll document the damage, provide all necessary paperwork, and work with your adjuster to ensure you get fair compensation.

What types of roofing materials do you offer?

We install architectural asphalt shingles, standing seam metal roofing, flat roof systems (TPO, EPDM, modified bitumen), and more. We'll help you choose the best material for your home, budget, and the Pacific Northwest climate.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential roof replacements are completed in 1-3 days, depending on the size and complexity of your roof. We work efficiently while maintaining quality standards, and we'll give you a clear timeline before starting.

Do you offer financing options?

Yes! We offer flexible financing with payments as low as $97 per month. We also accept insurance claims. Contact us for details on available financing plans.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured?

Yes. Kitsap Roof Pros is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in the State of Washington. We carry comprehensive liability coverage and workers' compensation for your complete peace of mind.

What areas do you serve?

We serve all of Kitsap County including Bremerton, Silverdale, Port Orchard, Poulsbo, Bainbridge Island, Kingston, and surrounding communities.

Do you offer any workmanship guarantees or warranties?

Yes. Every roof replacement we perform comes with our workmanship warranty, in addition to manufacturer warranties on all materials. We stand behind our work and your satisfaction is guaranteed.
