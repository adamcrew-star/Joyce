FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my roof needs repair or replacement?

If the damage is localized—a few missing shingles, a single leak, or minor flashing issues—a repair is usually the best option. If your roof is over 20 years old or has widespread damage, replacement may be more cost-effective. We offer free inspections to give you an honest recommendation.

Do you offer emergency roof repair?

Yes! We provide 24/7 emergency roof repair service. If you have an active leak or storm damage that needs immediate attention, call us and we'll dispatch a crew as quickly as possible to protect your home.

Can you help me with an insurance claim?

Absolutely. We work with insurance companies regularly and can help guide you through the claims process. We'll document the damage, provide all necessary paperwork, and work with your adjuster to ensure you get fair compensation.

How long does a typical roof repair take?

Most repairs are completed in a single day, often within a few hours. Larger repairs may take 1-2 days. We'll give you a clear timeline before starting any work.

What types of roofs do you repair?

We repair all types of residential and commercial roofing including asphalt shingles, metal roofs, flat roofs (TPO, EPDM), cedar shakes, and more.

Do you offer any warranties on repairs?

Yes. Every repair comes with our workmanship warranty. We stand behind our work and your satisfaction is guaranteed.

What areas do you serve?

We serve all of Pierce County including Tacoma, Lakewood, Puyallup, University Place, Edgewood, Sumner, Bonney Lake, Auburn, Kent, Parkland, Spanaway, and surrounding communities.

How much does a roof repair cost?

Repair costs vary depending on the type and extent of damage. Minor repairs may start at a few hundred dollars, while larger repairs can be more. We provide free, no-obligation estimates so you know exactly what to expect before any work begins.
