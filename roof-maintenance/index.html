FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have my roof maintained?

We recommend a professional roof inspection and maintenance at least once a year, ideally in spring or fall. In the Pacific Northwest, twice-yearly maintenance is ideal due to heavy rain and moss growth.

What does a roof maintenance visit include?

A typical visit includes a full roof inspection, debris removal, moss treatment, gutter cleaning, minor repairs (loose shingles, cracked caulk), and a detailed condition report with photos.

Can maintenance really extend my roof's lifespan?

Absolutely. Regular maintenance can add 5-10 years to your roof's lifespan by catching and addressing small issues before they cause major damage. It's one of the best investments you can make in your home.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Yes! We offer seasonal maintenance programs that include scheduled visits, priority service, and discounted rates on any repairs needed. Contact us for plan details and pricing.

Is moss on my roof really a problem?

Yes. Moss traps moisture against your shingles, causing them to deteriorate faster. Over time, this leads to leaks and premature roof failure. Regular moss treatment is essential in the Pacific Northwest climate.

What areas do you serve?

We serve all of Pierce County including Tacoma, Lakewood, Puyallup, University Place, Edgewood, Sumner, Bonney Lake, Auburn, Kent, Parkland, Spanaway, and surrounding communities.

How much does roof maintenance cost?

Maintenance costs vary depending on roof size and condition. Basic maintenance visits typically start at a very affordable rate. We provide free estimates so you know exactly what to expect.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Pierce Roof Pros is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in the State of Washington. We carry comprehensive liability coverage and workers' compensation for your complete peace of mind.
