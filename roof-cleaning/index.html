FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is roof cleaning really necessary?

Yes. Moss, algae, and debris trap moisture against your roofing materials, causing premature deterioration. Regular cleaning can extend your roof's life by years and prevent costly repairs.

Will pressure washing damage my roof?

We use low-pressure washing techniques specifically designed for roofing materials. Unlike high-pressure washing, our methods effectively clean without damaging shingles, tiles, or metal roofing.

How often should I have my roof cleaned?

In the Pacific Northwest, we recommend roof cleaning every 1-2 years depending on the amount of shade and moisture your roof receives. Homes surrounded by trees may need more frequent cleaning.

Do you apply preventative treatments?

Yes! After cleaning, we can apply zinc or copper-based treatments that inhibit moss and algae growth, keeping your roof cleaner for longer between professional cleanings.

How long does roof cleaning take?

Most residential roof cleanings are completed in 3-6 hours depending on roof size and the amount of buildup. We'll give you a clear timeline during your free estimate.

What areas do you serve?

We serve all of Pierce County including Tacoma, Lakewood, Puyallup, University Place, Edgewood, Sumner, Bonney Lake, Auburn, Kent, Parkland, Spanaway, and surrounding communities.

How much does roof cleaning cost?

Costs depend on your roof's size, pitch, and the amount of buildup. We provide free, detailed estimates so you know exactly what to expect. Most residential cleanings are very affordable.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Pierce Roof Pros is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in Washington State. We carry comprehensive liability and workers' comp coverage for your complete protection.
